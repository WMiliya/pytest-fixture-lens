# Code of Conduct

We want FixtureLens to be a welcoming, practical project for pytest users and contributors.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Help newcomers learn the codebase.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing secrets, proprietary test data, or private reports in issues or PRs.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; or block contributors who violate this code. Report problems privately through the repository maintainers.
